# Twin Cities Events: August 11 – August 25, 2026

This roundup covers **39 events** across the Twin Cities over the next two weeks, drawn from Saint Paul Public Library, Ramsey County Library, and Minneapolis-area Eventbrite listings. Library programming carries most of the calendar — sewing, laser engraving, screenprinting, crochet, tech help, and a recurring run of book clubs and D&D nights across branches — while the Eventbrite side adds a handful of nightlife and music picks: An Evening with FINK at The Cedar (Aug 14), the queer patio party Indecent Exposure: Slow Burn (Aug 15), Tilia Augtoberfest headlined by of Montreal (Aug 16), and Daily Stoic Live with Ryan Holiday at Ted Mann Concert Hall (Aug 18). Weekend daytime hours lean toward casual hobbyist meetups — crosswords, poetry, women's networking, and a cannabis growers competition. It's a fairly quiet, low-key stretch overall, best suited to routine drop-in programming rather than big one-off happenings.
