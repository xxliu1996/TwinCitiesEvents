# Twin Cities Events: October 22 – November 5, 2026

## Summary

The next two weeks bring **94 events** across Saint Paul and Minneapolis, with Halloween as the dominant theme through the weekend of Oct 31 — think the BOOnion Depot family festival, the Great Pumpkin Halloween Festival at Landmark Center, Gravezig's annual Halloween Bash, a reggaeton rave, multiple drag brunches and drag shows, and the Dresden Dolls' 25th-anniversary tour stopping at the Palace Theatre. Live music and comedy are well represented at Amsterdam Bar and Hall, MetroNOME Brewery, and the Fitzgerald Theater (Jeremy Messersmith, Randy Feltface, Greg Freeman), while Como Zoo Conservatory keeps its usual daily rhythm of keeper talks, gorilla and polar bear programs, and water garden tours throughout the window. Saint Paul Public Library and Ramsey County Library branches add a steady stream of adult book clubs, craft nights, and author events. The period quiets down noticeably after Halloween weekend, tapering to a handful of library, Como Zoo, and neighborhood events through the first week of November. Sources: Como Zoo Conservatory, Eventbrite, Minneapolis.org, Ramsey County Library, Saint Paul Public Library, Visit Saint Paul.
