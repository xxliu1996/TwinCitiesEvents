# Twin Cities Events: October 1 – October 15, 2026

This two-week window is packed with live music and nightlife, led by a strong concert run at the Palace Theatre and Fitzgerald Theater (They Might Be Giants, Zach Top, JOURNEY, Snail Mail & Soccer Mommy, UB40) alongside Minneapolis club shows and drag/burlesque nights. Fall and Halloween themes dominate the weekend festivals — the Twin Cities Bloody Mary Festival, Darkness Day at Surly, Meritage Oysterfest, and the Glass Pumpkin Patch — plus a steady calendar of adult-oriented library programs (tarot, D&D, book clubs, craft workshops) and daily Como Zoo Conservatory keeper talks and animal programs. Sports fans get Minnesota United FC home games and a US vs. Canada international friendly at Allianz Field, and runners/cyclists have the Twin Cities Bike Tour and Spooky Sprint 5K. In total, **139 events** made the cut after filtering for timing and adult-appropriateness, spanning six sources across Saint Paul and Minneapolis.
