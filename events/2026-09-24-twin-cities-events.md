# Twin Cities Events: September 24 – October 8, 2026

This roundup covers **127 events** across the Twin Cities over the next two weeks, pulled from Saint Paul and Ramsey County libraries, Como Park Zoo & Conservatory, Visit Saint Paul, Minneapolis.org, and Eventbrite. The opening weekend (Sept 25–27) is the busiest stretch, packed with multiple Oktoberfest celebrations (Utepils, Brühaven, Wandering Leaf), the Teddy Swims and Johnny Blue Skies concerts at Grand Casino Arena, the Twin City Cat Fanciers CFA Cat Show and POP Cats Convention at Union Depot and Saint Paul RiverCentre, and Como Zoo's recurring "Gorillas on the Line" weekend programming. Como Zoo contributes a steady stream of free daily programs (Polar Bear talks, giraffe feedings, gorilla programs) throughout the whole period, while the libraries offer craft nights, book clubs, and skill-building classes (knitting, tarot, laser engraving) mostly on weekday evenings. Live music is well represented with Bleachers at The Armory, Bruce Hornsby & The Noisemakers, and Beth Hart at the Palace Theatre, alongside a handful of drag shows, comedy sets, and neighborhood festivals like Marydale Festival and Open Streets Nicollet.
