# Twin Cities Events: November 5 – November 19, 2026

The next two weeks in the Twin Cities lean heavily on live music and comedy at Visit Saint Paul venues, a steady drumbeat of Como Zoo Conservatory nature programs (polar bears, gorillas, bonsai, aquatic encounters), and library-driven book clubs, craft nights, and maker workshops from Saint Paul Public Library and Ramsey County Library. Standout one-offs include the **Twin Cities Book Festival** and **Collectors Day 2026** on Nov 7, the **Marigold de Muertos: A Day of the Dead Celebration** and **Picturegoer Film Club's "Who Framed Roger Rabbit"** screening, the **Moth: Home for the Holidays Story Slam** (Nov 10), and the season's first **Tree of Lights Display at Rice Park** lighting up Nov 14. Nightlife and dating scenes are well represented too, with several 21+/30+ singles and speed-dating mixers on Eventbrite alongside wrestling, roller derby, and a handful of album-release shows. In total, **131 events** made the cut after filtering out daytime-weekday and kids-only listings — plenty to fill out two weekends and several weeknight evenings.

## Sources with issues

- **Still Water Events** (iCalendar feed at `events.discoverstillwater.com`) failed to fetch: `Remote end closed connection without response`. This is **not** a network-allowlist problem — `curl` reaches the same URL through the same proxy in under a second and gets a clean `200` with the full ICS payload. The server negotiates ALPN `h2` and appears to drop plain HTTP/1.1 connections (what Python's `urllib`/`http.client` speaks), closing the socket without a response. The script's fetch helper doesn't negotiate HTTP/2, so this source consistently fails while still exiting cleanly (exit code 0) — per instructions, left `scripts/fetch_events.py` unmodified since it didn't error out. Stillwater-area events are therefore missing from this week's list.
