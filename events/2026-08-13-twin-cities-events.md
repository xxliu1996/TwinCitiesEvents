# Twin Cities Events: August 13 – August 27, 2026

This roundup covers **39 events** across Saint Paul, Ramsey County, and Minneapolis over the next two weeks. Library programming dominates the list — crafts, board games, book clubs, and maker-space drop-ins spread across branches like George Latimer Central, Dayton's Bluff, and Roseville — with the busiest days being Saturday, August 15 and Saturday, August 22, when library weekend programming clusters alongside Eventbrite listings. On the nightlife and music side, look for FINK at The Cedar Cultural Center, a backyard show from The OK Factor + The High 48s, drag brunch at Roxy's Cabaret, and the queer patio party Indecent Exposure at Icehouse. Book lovers have several club meetups (Shoreview Mystery, North St. Paul, St. Anthony Park) plus the Midwest Black Book Fair on August 22. A handful of clearly kids-only library programs (FunLab, a dog-reading program, a caregiver-and-kids clay event) were filtered out of this list.
