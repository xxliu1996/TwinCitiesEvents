# Twin Cities Events: July 30 – August 13, 2026

This roundup covers **38 events** across the Twin Cities over the next two weeks, drawn from Saint Paul Public Library, Ramsey County Library, and Minneapolis-area Eventbrite listings. The lineup leans heavily on free library programming — sewing and mending workshops, laser engraving, tech help, book clubs, birding, and yoga — alongside a handful of nightlife and creative happenings like drag brunches and shows, a comedy night, an antifascist benefit hootenanny, a silent party, and a couple of book launches. The first weekend (Aug 1–2) is the busiest stretch, with a food truck festival and a fashion pop-up bookending a wide mix of library drop-ins. Also worth flagging: Ramsey County early voting opens at the Roseville library on Aug 8 for residents who want to get it done early.
