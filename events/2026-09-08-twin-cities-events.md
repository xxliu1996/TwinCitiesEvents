# Twin Cities Events: September 8 – September 22, 2026

One hundred thirty-two events made the cut this fortnight, and Saturday, September 12 is by far the biggest day on the calendar — a Vikings-kickoff-weekend pile-up of SkolFest, a Fan Appreciation Night Saints game, three separate drag shows, live sets from Bettye LaVette and Bob Mould's SUGAR (playing a rare three-night run at First Avenue), plus a full slate of daytime library workshops and Como Zoo talks. Beyond that peak, the two weeks are anchored by steady library programming (a dozen-plus book clubs, laser engraving, sewing, bookbinding, and D&D nights across Saint Paul and Ramsey County branches) and Como Zoo Conservatory's recurring daily talks (Polar Bear, Gorilla, Gardener, and Fish Chat programs). Music and nightlife skew toward Minneapolis venues like First Avenue, the Dakota, and the Fillmore, while Saint Paul's second week brings a run of neighborhood festivals — Frogtown Arts Festival, Capital City Fest, CLUES Fiesta Latina — plus a big-ticket Weezer/The Shins/Silversun Pickups show closing out the period. Literary highlights include two Subtext Books author events and a Red Balloon Bookshop appearance by Elizabeth Acevedo on the final Monday. Note that nine Visit Saint Paul listings (Wakpá Triennial, Mexican Independence Day Celebration, Capital City Fest, the Weezer show, and others) have no specific start time in the source feed — these are flagged "Time TBD" below and are likely day-long or multi-session events.
