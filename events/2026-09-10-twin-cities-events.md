# Twin Cities Events: September 10 – September 24, 2026

129 events made the cut over the next two weeks, with the opening weekend (Sept 11-13) the clear high point: Vikings-kickoff-weekend energy (SkolFest, a Kickoff Concert, tailgates), the Wakpá Triennial Art Festival, Selby Ave JazzFest, and the Mexican Independence Day Celebration all converge on Saturday the 12th, alongside a run of drag shows and Bob Mould's SUGAR playing First Avenue two nights running. Beyond that peak, steady programming carries the rest of the fortnight — Como Zoo Conservatory's daily Polar Bear, Gorilla, Gardener, and Fish Chat talks, a dozen-plus library book clubs and craft nights across Saint Paul and Ramsey County branches, and Minneapolis/Saint Paul live music at First Avenue, the Dakota, and the Fillmore. The second weekend brings another cluster of neighborhood festivals (Frogtown Arts Festival, Capital City Fest, CLUES Fiesta Latina, Twin Cities Veg Fest) plus a Weezer/The Shins/Silversun Pickups show at Grand Casino Arena to close out the period.
