# Twin Cities Events: November 12 – November 26, 2026

Two weeks of live music, comedy, markets, and library/museum programming across Saint Paul and Minneapolis — 122 events found. The slate leans heavily toward live music and comedy at Amsterdam Bar and Hall, MetroNOME Brewery, and TicketSmarter venues, alongside a steady drumbeat of free daily programming at Como Zoo & Conservatory (zookeeper talks, gorilla and polar bear programs, bonsai) and Saint Paul/Ramsey County library events (book clubs, craft nights, author talks). The weekend of November 14–15 is especially packed, and the holiday season kicks off in earnest around November 21 with the Tree of Lights display at Rice Park, the Schmidt Holiday Market, and the MN Christmas Market all getting underway. Notable one-offs include the Mall of America's "Hunger Games" costume contest (Nov 13), the Minnesota Roller Derby season opener (Nov 14), and touring acts like Russell Dickerson and Amythyst Kiah. Things thin out heading into Thanksgiving week (Nov 23–25), with mostly recurring library programs on the calendar.
