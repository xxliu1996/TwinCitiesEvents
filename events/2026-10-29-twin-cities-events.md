# Twin Cities Events: October 29 – November 12, 2026

## Summary

The next two weeks bring **109 events** across Saint Paul and Minneapolis, heavily front-loaded around Halloween weekend (Oct 30–31), which brings a wave of 21+ parties, drag brunches and dinners (Hocus Pocus, Sips & Slays, Roxy's Cabaret), costume balls, a reggaeton rave, Club Science: Halloween at the Science Museum, and big-name shows from Phillip Phillips and Ella Langley. Como Zoo Conservatory keeps its reliable daily rhythm of keeper talks, gorilla and polar bear programs, and water garden tours straight through the window, plus a one-week Ikebana Flower Show starting Nov 7. The period quiets noticeably after Halloween, settling into a steady trickle of Saint Paul and Ramsey County library programs — book clubs, craft nights, a grief-support group, and a meditation talk — alongside a few larger draws: the Jonas Brothers at Grand Casino Arena (Nov 2), the Twin Cities Book Festival (Nov 7), and the Turkey Trails 5K/10K (Nov 7). Live music and comedy fill out weeknights at venues like MetroNOME Brewery, Gambit Brewing, and Laugh Camp Comedy Club. Sources: Como Zoo Conservatory, Eventbrite, Minneapolis.org, Ramsey County Library, Saint Paul Public Library, Visit Saint Paul.
