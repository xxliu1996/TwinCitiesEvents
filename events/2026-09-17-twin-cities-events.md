# Twin Cities Events: September 17 – October 1, 2026

124 events made the cut over the next two weeks, led by a heavy wave of touring live music — Weezer/The Shins/Silversun Pickups at Grand Casino Arena, Role Model, The Rasmus, Logic & G-Eazy, and Mac McAnally at the Dakota. Opening weekend (Sept 18-20) is the busiest stretch, stacking neighborhood festivals (Frogtown Arts Festival, Capital City Fest, MinnesoThai Street Food Festival, CLUES Fiesta Latina, Twin Cities Veg Fest) with drag and comedy nights (Sips & Slays, The Goddess Experience, Funny Bachelors) and the Statewide Star Party at the Bell Museum. After that, things settle into steady programming: Como Zoo Conservatory's daily keeper talks, giraffe feedings, and gorilla/polar bear programs (plus a "Gorillas on the Line" weekend Sept 26-27), a run of Saint Paul and Ramsey County library book clubs, craft nights, and author events (Elizabeth Acevedo, Brian Duren), and the Marydale Festival and Beth Hart at the Palace Theatre closing out the fortnight.
