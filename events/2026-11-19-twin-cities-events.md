# Twin Cities Events: November 20 – December 3, 2026

This week's roundup has 109 events across the metro, running Friday, November 20 through Thursday, December 3. The run-up to Thanksgiving and the start of the holiday season dominate the calendar: look for early holiday markets and pop-ups (Schmidt Holiday Market, MN Christmas Market, A Handmade Holiday Market, Winter Market Series at Wandering Leaf), the kickoff of the Festive Holiday Lights Tour, and a stacked live-music lineup at First Avenue, the Dakota, Amsterdam Bar and Hall, and MetroNOME Brewery. Theater and comedy fans have plenty of choices too — Chanhassen's Carpenters tribute, *The Bald and the Beautiful* with Trixie and Katya, several Laugh Camp Comedy Club nights, and the Walker's annual Choreographers' Evening. Como Zoo Conservatory's daily keeper talks and animal programs run nearly every day of the window, and Saint Paul and Ramsey County libraries offer a steady stream of free workshops, book clubs, and craft sessions. Black Friday (Nov. 27) brings a late-night 18+ option alongside an all-ages indie show, and the week wraps with a handful of author talks and discussion groups at Subtext Books.
