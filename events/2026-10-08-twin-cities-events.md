# Twin Cities Events: October 8 – October 22, 2026

## Summary

The next two weeks bring **133 events** across Saint Paul and Minneapolis, spanning live music, festivals, Halloween-season fun, and a steady stream of adult-focused library programming. The opening weekend (Oct 9–11) is the busiest stretch, headlined by Indigenous People's Weekend and the Owámni: Falling Water Festival, the Autumn Brew Review beer fest, the Cine Latino Film Festival, and big-name concerts (UB40, Zach Top, Snail Mail & Soccer Mommy, Carbon Leaf). Halloween is already creeping in with the Spooky Sprint 5K/10K, Shadows & Spirits of the State Capitol tour, Halloween Pottery Paint & Sip, and a Hocus Pocus-themed drag dinner. Como Zoo Conservatory runs daily keeper talks, gorilla and polar bear programs, and water garden tours throughout the window, while Saint Paul Public Library and Ramsey County Library branches offer frequent adult book clubs, tarot classes, D&D nights, and author events (including Ramsey County's early voting kicking off Oct 17). The back half of the period (Oct 19–22) quiets down into mostly smaller, recurring library and community gatherings.
