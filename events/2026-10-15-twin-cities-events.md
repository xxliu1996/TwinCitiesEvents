# Twin Cities Events: October 15 – October 29, 2026

## Summary

The next two weeks bring **117 events** across Saint Paul and Minneapolis, with Halloween and "spooky season" as the dominant theme right up through Oct 29 — think the Jack-O'-Lantern Competition, Zombie Pub Crawl, Monster Burlesque, Hocus Pocus Drag Brunch, two separate Bizarre Bazaar art fairs, Lanterns & Legends at Historic Fort Snelling, a Ghostland bus tour, and Jeremy Messersmith's All Hallows' Eve show at the Fitzgerald. Opening weekend (Oct 16–18) is the busiest stretch, headlined by touring acts like Judah & the Lion, Big Head Todd and The Monsters, and Toad the Wet Sprocket, alongside the Saint Paul Chamber Orchestra's Haydn program and a steady run of comedy shows. Como Zoo Conservatory keeps its usual daily rhythm of keeper talks, gorilla and polar bear programs, and water garden tours throughout the window, while Saint Paul Public Library and Ramsey County Library branches offer frequent adult book clubs, craft nights, tarot classes, and author events — plus Ramsey County early voting running at the Shoreview branch starting Oct 17. The back half of the period (Oct 20–29) quiets into smaller recurring library and neighborhood gatherings, with a late surge of nightlife for Halloween week itself (ACOTAR Midnight Release Party, Trick-or-Takeover). Note: the Still Water Events calendar failed to load this week (see Sources with issues below), so St. Croix Valley-area events are not reflected here.
