# Twin Cities Events: September 7 – September 21, 2026

Forty-six adult-oriented events made the cut this fortnight, and library programming carries most of the load — Saint Paul Public Library and Ramsey County Library between them offer a dense slate of book clubs (six different clubs across the two weeks, from Highland Park to Books & Bars at Broken Clock Brewing), craft/maker workshops (laser engraving, sewing, bookbinding, Hmong needlework), and quieter drop-ins like Puzzle Night and Coffee and Crosswords. Saturday, September 12 is the standout day, stacking a full slate of daytime library workshops with an evening of Minneapolis nightlife — SkolFest's Vikings kickoff party, a Glitz & Glam drag show, stand-up comedy, and a Latin/Arabic dance debut at Union Rooftop. Literary highlights bookend the second week: a DOOMSDATE book release party on the 18th and a Red Balloon Bookshop event with bestselling author Elizabeth Acevedo on the 21st. Four of this week's six data sources (Still Water Events, Como Zoo Conservatory, Visit Saint Paul, and Minneapolis.org) failed to fetch due to a sandbox network restriction rather than any change on the sites themselves — see "Sources with issues" below — so this digest likely undercounts outdoor, tourism, and conservatory-style events compared to a normal week.

## Sources with issues

Four of the six sources failed to fetch this week, all with the same `403 Forbidden` / tunnel-connection error: Still Water Events, Como Zoo Conservatory, Visit Saint Paul, and Minneapolis.org. This is the sandbox's own outbound network allowlist blocking these domains, not a change on the sites or a script bug — `README.md` already documents that these four domains need to be added to the environment's allowlist alongside the two that did succeed (`gateway.bibliocommons.com`, `www.eventbrite.com`). These four sources have not appeared in any prior weekly digest in this repo's history either, so this is a standing gap, not a new regression — but it's worth fixing the allowlist so future digests include Stillwater-area events, the Como Zoo Conservatory calendar, and Visit Saint Paul/Minneapolis.org tourism listings.

**Note:** 6 listings that passed the automated adult/kids-keyword filter were manually removed as clear misses during review — four "FunLab" STEAM drop-ins (ages 7–11), a "Saturday Vibe – Mini Maker" family STEAM session, and a "Paw Pals" dog reading program (ages 6–12) — since none contained the filter's kid-related keywords despite being explicitly age-gated for children or families.

Two informational warnings (not fetch failures) were also logged for a duplicate non-Minnesota Eventbrite listing ("Move Your Chapter Forward: Parliamentary Skills That Empower Every Member") that the region filter correctly excluded.
