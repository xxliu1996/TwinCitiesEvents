# Twin Cities Events: September 3 – September 17, 2026

Thirty-eight events made the cut this fortnight, split fairly evenly between free/low-key Saint Paul and Ramsey County library programming and ticketed Minneapolis nightlife/concert listings on Eventbrite. Book clubs are especially well represented — six different clubs meet across the two weeks, from Riverview and Highland Park to Books & Bars at Broken Clock Brewing. Weekend highlights include a Saturday night out on 9/5 (live music, a "Metropolis" film screening, a book launch party) and a busy Saturday on 9/12 with a comedy show, a rooftop Latin/Arabic dance debut, and a full slate of daytime library workshops (sewing, laser engraving, D&D, a historical building tour). Craft and maker programming continues to be a library strength — mending, laser engraving, bookbinding, and needlework classes all appear. Five library listings aimed squarely at kids (ages 6–12 reading program, two "FunLab" sessions for ages 7–11 recurring on both Saturdays) were filtered out as clearly not adult events despite passing the automated keyword filter.
